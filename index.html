Meet Jeffrey Bowen

Jeffrey Bowen is a broker associate with ERealty Advisors and leads The Bowen Realty Group out of 9 Canal Street in Boston. He has been licensed in Massachusetts for 24 years, and in that time the group has closed 716 sales — the large majority of them in Chelsea, East Boston, Everett, Malden and Revere.

That concentration is the point. Jeffrey is not covering the whole metro. He knows which Chelsea loft buildings have healthy reserves, what a triple-decker on the wrong side of a street actually trades for, and which buyers are already looking on a given block. It is the kind of knowledge that only comes from selling the same neighborhoods for two decades.

His work splits fairly evenly between listing homes, representing buyers, and rentals and small commercial deals. He handles a steady share of first-time buyers, and works in English and Spanish — which matters in a city where most households speak Spanish at home.

Where Jeffrey works

Five cities, all within about four miles of each other, each with its own pricing logic.

Chelsea

Converted mill lofts, newer waterfront condos along Boatswains Way, and dense two- and three-family homes. Jeffrey’s home market and the source of most of his listings.

East Boston

Triple-deckers and condo conversions with harbor and skyline views, plus multifamily buildings held by long-time owner-occupants.

Everett

Single-families and two-families that draw buyers priced out of Somerville and Charlestown, with steady demand near the Orange Line.

Malden

Larger lots and more single-family stock than its neighbors, which is where much of the family-sized inventory in this price band sits.

Revere

Beachfront condos alongside inland two-families, with a wider spread between the top and bottom of the market than buyers expect.

Boston

Condos and rentals across the city through ERealty Advisors, for clients moving in or out of the neighborhoods above.

The Chelsea loft buildings
