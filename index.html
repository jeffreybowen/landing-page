Where Jeffrey works

Chelsea and surrounding Greater Boston communities. Ask Jeffrey about representation for your particular property and location.

Chelsea

Converted mill lofts, newer waterfront condos along Boatswains Way, and dense two- and three-family homes. Jeffrey’s home market and the source of most of his listings.

East Boston

Triple-deckers and condo conversions with harbor and skyline views, plus multifamily buildings held by long-time owner-occupants.

Everett

Single-families and two-families that draw buyers priced out of Somerville and Charlestown, with steady demand near the Orange Line.

Malden

Larger lots and more single-family stock than its neighbors, which is where much of the family-sized inventory in this price band sits.

Revere

Beachfront condos alongside inland two-families, with a wider spread between the top and bottom of the market than buyers expect.

Boston

Condos and rentals across the city through ERealty Advisors, for clients moving in or out of the neighborhoods above.
