What to discuss at your first meeting

Your timing

When would you like to move? Do you need to buy another property, coordinate with occupants or plan work before listing?

Your property

Bring questions about condition, improvements, parking and ownership documents. Share known issues so the right professionals can help.

Your options

Discuss comparable sales, preparation priorities, marketing services and the terms of representation before making a decision.

Preparing a Chelsea condo for sale

Individual buildings can have different documents, fees and rules. Gather what you have and confirm current information with the association or property manager.

  • Condominium documents, current fees and any known special assessments.
  • Information about parking, storage and what belongs to the unit.
  • Available records of improvements, maintenance and warranties.
  • Questions about access, photography, showings and your moving schedule.

For a multifamily or tenant-occupied property, discuss occupancy and access with Jeffrey and your attorney before planning a launch. Do not send leases, financial records or other sensitive documents through the general inquiry form.

From preparation to closing

  1. Plan: discuss goals, representation, compensation and property preparation.
  2. Launch: confirm property information and approve photographs and marketing.
  3. Review offers: compare price, financing, timing and other terms with professional guidance.
  4. Complete the transaction: coordinate inspections, legal documents, financing and closing with the responsible professionals.

The exact sequence and deadlines depend on your transaction. Read the Massachusetts transaction overview and obtain legal advice before signing binding agreements.

Questions sellers often ask

How will we discuss an asking price?

Ask Jeffrey to explain the relevant comparable sales, competing listings and property-specific differences. An initial discussion is not an appraisal or a guarantee of the eventual sale price.

Do I need to renovate before listing?

Discuss preparation costs, timing and likely buyer questions before committing to work. The right approach depends on the property and your priorities; renovation does not guarantee a particular return.

Can I talk with Jeffrey before deciding to sell?

Yes. Start with your questions and approximate timing, and discuss the available services and any applicable terms directly with Jeffrey.

What does representation cost?

Compensation and services are negotiable and should be explained in the representation agreement. Ask Jeffrey to walk you through the scope and terms.
