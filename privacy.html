Privacy & contacting Jeffrey

Information you choose to share

The inquiry form asks for your name, email and optional telephone number, along with your real estate interests and message. These details are used to respond to your request. Submitting an inquiry does not subscribe you to ongoing marketing.

How an inquiry is delivered

The text beside the form explains its current delivery method. In email-draft mode, nothing is submitted by the website: you must send the draft through your email provider. When secure form delivery is enabled, Formspree processes the submission for the business. Campaign labels and the page path may accompany a submitted inquiry to help identify its source.

External services

Property and review links open external websites. Booking, when available, uses Calendly. Video playback loads YouTube when you select a video. These providers have their own privacy policies. Fonts and property or video images may be requested from external providers when pages load.

Website measurement

When enabled, Google Analytics measures visits and interactions such as contact clicks and confirmed form submissions. The website does not intentionally send your form name, email, phone or message to Analytics. Applicable consent and privacy settings must be maintained by the website operator.

Your choices

You can call or email Jeffrey instead of using the form. Avoid sharing sensitive financial, identity or legal documents here. For questions about personal information you have shared, or to request correction or deletion, contact Info@chelseaRealestate.com. Applicable recordkeeping requirements may affect what can be deleted.
